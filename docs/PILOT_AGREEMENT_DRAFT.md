## 4. Using Relay
- Use it lawfully and do not send spam or abuse. We may stop access that puts others or the service at risk.
- Keep your sign-in details and invite code private. People who join your workspace are your responsibility.

You may tell us what you think. We may use your feedback to improve Relay without paying you or crediting you.
